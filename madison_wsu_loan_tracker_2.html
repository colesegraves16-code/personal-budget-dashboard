Rules That Directly Affect Madison
🔑

Rule 1 — Graduate PLUS Loans Eliminated for New Borrowers July 1, 2026

Starting July 1, 2026, Graduate PLUS loans are unavailable to students who haven't already borrowed federally in their current program. Madison secures access by getting the Summer 2026 unsubsidized loan disbursed before that date, making her a "legacy borrower."

→ FSA NSLDS Update — Grad PLUS elimination and legacy exception (fsapartners.ed.gov)
📅

Rule 2 — The July 1, 2026 Deadline Is Written Into Federal Law

The loan must be disbursed (money actually sent to the school) — not just applied for, approved, or certified. P.L. 119-21 sets this date and WSU cannot extend it. Per NASFAA's confirmed reading, certification alone does not qualify — only disbursement does.

→ NASFAA OBBBA summary — disbursement (not certification) is the trigger
→ WSU Financial Aid News — check for local disbursement timeline
🎓

Rule 3 — Legacy Status Lasts Up to 3 Award Years

Once established, Madison can access Graduate PLUS for up to 3 award years or until she completes the DPT program — whichever comes first. The DPT is a 3-year program, so she is covered the full duration as long as she maintains continuous enrollment.

→ FSA NSLDS Update — legacy exception period details
🚫

Rule 4 — Withdrawal or Unapproved Leave Terminates Legacy Status

Withdrawing from any term or taking an unapproved leave risks losing legacy status permanently. Only an approved leave under federal regulation 34 CFR 668.22 is protected. Confirm with WSU Financial Aid before any break from enrollment.

→ FSA OBBBA guidance on enrollment continuity and legacy status
💰

Rule 5 — $20,500 Annual Cap Per Award Year (July 1 – June 30)

Graduate students (including DPT) are capped at $20,500/yr in unsubsidized loans. Summer, Fall, and Spring all draw from the same pool for that award year. Summer 2026 is the exception — in AY 2025–26 with its own separate cap that doesn't affect future years.

→ FSA DL-25-03 — 2025–26 loan limits and rates
📊

Rule 6 — $257,500 Lifetime Borrowing Limit (All Federal Loans)

All federal student loans — undergrad and graduate, including Graduate PLUS — count toward a $257,500 lifetime limit. Madison currently has ~$15,011 in bachelor's principal, leaving ~$242,489 before DPT loans begin. Graduate PLUS counts toward this per the final OBBBA rule issued May 2026.

→ NASFAA: Final OBBBA Rule — Grad PLUS included in $257,500 lifetime limit
🏥

Rule 7 — DPT Is Graduate, Not Professional — $20,500 Cap Applies

Under OBBBA, law, medicine, dentistry, and theology are "professional" programs at $50,000/yr. Physical therapy does not meet the federal regulatory definition (34 CFR 668.2) at this time. WSU Financial Aid has confirmed this. Madison gets $20,500/yr, not $50,000/yr.

→ NASFAA RISE Committee Q&A — DPT and professional program classification
📋

Rule 8 — New Repayment Plans for Loans After July 1, 2026

DPT loans (all after July 1, 2026) can only use the new Tiered Standard plan or Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP). Old plans (SAVE, PAYE, ICR) are eliminated for these loans. Bachelor's loans (before July 1, 2026) still have old plan access until July 2028.

→ studentaid.gov — all repayment plan options
→ FSA GEN-25-04 — repayment changes under OBBBA
